# Verification results

Generated by `python -m bvm verify`. Deterministic (fixed seed); rerun after any engine change.
Supports evidence categories 1 and 8 of the credibility assessment plan ([credibility-assessment-plan.md](credibility-assessment-plan.md)).

## Code verification

`tests/test_verification.py` checks the solver against closed-form solutions and theory:

* uniform rigid tube: pressure drop, WSS and velocity equal Hagen–Poiseuille to 1e-12
* linear taper: matches the closed-form integral, with observed order 2.0 (trapezoid rule)
* rigid stenosis: equals the exact viscous integral plus the Young–Tsai loss to 1e-10
* elastic wall: second-order convergence; inlet strain equals the Laplace thin-wall value
* White curvature correlation: continuous at De = 11.6 and monotonic
* territory BC reduces to a series-resistance circuit; branched vessels conserve mass

## Calculation verification: grid convergence

Grids of 201, 401, 801 stations; GCI (safety factor 1.25) is the numerical error band on the 401-station result that the app and CLI report. `p` is the observed order.
Pressure and flow converge at second order or better. Peak WSS, throat Re and perforator flow converge at about first order, because a maximum is sampled at stations and branch origins snap to the nearest station; their GCI is still well under 1%. Orders far from 1–2 (or negative) occur when the grid differences are at round-off level, and the GCI there is negligible.

## Reading these numbers

* Numerical (grid) error is negligible next to input uncertainty, so credibility is limited by **model form and inputs**, which only validation (bench, in vivo, population) can address.
* These results verify that the equations are solved correctly. They do not show the equations are the right ones; see the validation activities in the plan.
