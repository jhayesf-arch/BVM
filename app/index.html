Downstream tissue

Why there are no sliders here

This geometry comes from a scan, so its shape is fixed. The lesions and treated lumens were cut into the scanned vessel when the files were built (70% plaque, 95% clot, and the app's device rules). The sliders in Parametric mode build an idealized tube, and they don't apply to a scanned vessel. To try other severities or positions, regenerate the files with scripts/cmha_variants.py.
