Self-test: JS engine and device logic

Verification evidence for the credibility plan. The engine runs closed-form checks and a robustness grid on both station counts the app uses. Every device runs across 8 vessel diameters, 4 stenoses and 4 lesion lengths, with successful and failed passes for thrombectomy devices. Each case is checked against the device's sizing rule, its outcome rule, physical sense (no worse lumen or flow) and its IFU flags. The Python engine is tested separately with pytest.
